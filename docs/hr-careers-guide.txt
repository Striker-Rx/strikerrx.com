StrikerRx careers publishing — proposed HR setup and instructions
Prepared October 16, 2026

STATUS
The five previous job listings and their detail pages have been removed from the
live website. The careers page now says there are no open roles. The site does
not currently have an HR editor. The workflow below is a proposed setup, not an
activated account or integration.

RECOMMENDED SETUP
Use HR's existing recruiting system if it offers a hosted careers page or a jobs
widget. Connect that page to StrikerRx once, then HR manages openings there.
If HR does not have a system, Breezy HR supports both a hosted careers portal and
an embeddable jobs list. Confirm the account, plan and HR access before setup.

ONE-TIME SETUP — HR AND WEBSITE ADMINISTRATOR
1. Confirm the recruiting system HR will own. The instructions below use Breezy.
2. Have the company administrator create/configure the company account and give
   the responsible HR staff Hiring Manager or Admin access as appropriate.
3. In Breezy, open Settings > Company Settings > Career Site. Add approved company
   branding and copy. Confirm which HR staff should receive and review applicants.
4. Send the public career portal URL to the website administrator. The simplest
   integration is a button on the StrikerRx careers page linking to that portal.
   To show the jobs directly on StrikerRx, an Admin can instead open Career Site >
   Embed Widget, choose grouping, and send its code for one-time installation.
5. Verify a real approved opening, its application form, and the closed-role
   behavior from the public StrikerRx website on desktop and mobile.
6. Once the integration is confirmed, use the steps below for each role. No
   website code changes or EC2 access should be needed for routine job updates.

POST A ROLE — AFTER BREEZY SETUP
1. Sign in with your HR account and open Positions/Pools.
2. Choose + Add Position.
3. Enter the title, employment type, location, and approved job description.
4. Complete the application form, hiring team, and remaining position settings.
   Check the career-site visibility and job-board settings before activating.
5. Choose Save as Draft for review, or Activate Position when ready. Complete any
   required internal approval. Active public roles appear on the career portal.
6. Visit the public careers page from StrikerRx and verify the title, description,
   location, and application link.

EDIT A ROLE
1. Open Positions/Pools and select the correct opening.
2. Use the gear icon at the top right to open Position Settings.
3. Update the content and save. Check the public posting again.

REMOVE A ROLE FROM OPENINGS
1. Open Positions/Pools and locate the role.
2. Open its status menu (colored dot) and choose Closed.
3. If prompted, select the closing reason. Review any candidate-disqualification
   options separately; closing a listing does not require a new hiring decision.
4. Check the public careers page to confirm the role is no longer an open posting.
5. Keep its records for HR's normal retention process. Archived hides a position
   internally; Delete permanently removes its data and is unnecessary for closing.

APPLICATIONS
Under this proposal, future role applications are collected in the recruiting
system. Existing website applications already stored in Snowflake remain there;
this setup does not migrate them. Agree who owns those historical applications
before switching intake.
