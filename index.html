About StrikerRx

The pharmacy behind the formulation.

Why this business exists

StrikerRx is a 503A-licensed sterile compounding pharmacy operating out of Katy, Texas. We prepare patient-specific medications for healthcare providers who need formulations not available through commercial manufacturing: different doses, different delivery forms, allergen-free alternatives, or combinations that simply do not exist at retail scale.

We work with providers across every specialty, from primary care and weight loss clinics to hormone therapy, medical spas, and telemedicine. Every compound we prepare is tied to a specific patient, a specific prescription, and specific clinical needs.

Team credentials

Our compounding staff bring more than 30 years of combined pharmaceutical experience to every preparation we make.
